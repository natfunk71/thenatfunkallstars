The Nat Funk All Stars

Original Hard Rock & Heavy Funk from New York

Band Overview

The Nat Funk All Stars are a high-decibel, original hard rock and heavy funk outfit rooted in New York. Built on a foundation of aggressive rhythm sections, soaring electric guitar work, and grinding Hammond B3 organ lines, the group delivers a distinct sonic experience that bridges late-60s heavy psychedelic funk with 70s hard rock grit.

Musical Pedigree & Related Projects

The musicians behind The Nat Funk All Stars bring extensive studio and stage credentials across multiple original and touring projects, including The Mutha Funkas, The Electric Fire Escape, No Mercy, The Blues Cousins, The Jeff Denny Band, and Tenebrae[cite: 1.2].

Over the years, members have shared the stage or collaborated with notable artists such as Jeff Denny, John Miceli, Bobby Rondinelli, Teddy Rondinelli, Jules Radino, Mike Clemente, Dave Barinoski, Candice Night, Frankie Desposito, Anton Fig, Tony Lepore, Cindy Lopez, and Todd Shea[cite: 1.2]. The band has also shared bills with acts like Ted Poley, The Scofflaws, and The Ice Cold Killers[cite: 1.2].

Production, Sound Engineering & Film Work

Beyond performance, the team brings heavy front-of-house live audio engineering and scoring expertise[cite: 1.2]. FOH engineering credentials span major venues such as Nassau Coliseum and the USS Intrepid, running live sound for artists and events including Sponge, Gilby Clarke, Murphy’s Law, Vince Neil, Dr. Jane Goodall, The Ataris, Mike DelGuidice, and the Islanders Hockey Bud Light Rock Zone[cite: 1.2].

The team has also composed and produced original music scores and sound cues for various Off-Broadway theatrical productions and indie films[cite: 1.2].

Discography & Featured Tracks

The band's catalog features a mix of heavy instrumental showcases and hard-hitting vocal tracks:

  • The Electric Fire Escape (EP) – Flagship release featuring full-band arrangements and driving organ leads.
  • The Coffee Truck Is Here – Syncopated bass and rhythm guitar interplay.
  • Nat Funk on the Organ – Overdriven organ improvisation and dynamic breakdown sections.
  • Blue Shoes Have No Place in Outer Space – Psychedelic hard-rock driven by space-age synth and heavy guitar riffs.
  • Nat Funk's Lament – Soulful blues-rock featuring deep dynamic range.
  • Mud Foot – Distorted bassline and driving drum pocket.
  • Suck The Poison Out – Fast-paced hard rock with aggressive vocal hooks.
  • Sneaky Pete – Mid-tempo funk-rock packed with organ comping and guitar fills.

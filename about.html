The Nat Funk All Stars

Original Hard Rock Funk from New York

Band Overview

The Nat Funk All Stars were formed by Nat Funk upon the dissolution of The Mutha Funkas circa 2004. The band is led by Nat Funk, who is the primary lyricist, arranger, and composer. He plays bass, guitar, Hammond Organ, Fender Rhodes, and programs all MIDI instruments, including drums and percussion. Loops and samples are sometimes employed as well. A rotating roster of friends and paid musicians play live shows and on most recordings if Funk is not the only featured musician. The band has also composed and produced original music scores and sound cues for various Off-Broadway theatrical productions and indie films.

Musical Pedigree & Related Projects

The musicians behind The Nat Funk All Stars bring extensive studio and stage credentials across multiple original and touring projects, including The Mutha Funkas, The Electric Fire Escape, The Jeff Denny Band, and Tenebrae.

Over the years, members have shared the stage or collaborated with notable artists such as Jeff Denny, John Miceli, Bobby Rondinelli, Teddy Rondinelli, Jules Radino, Mike Clemente, Dave Barinoski, Candice Night, Frankie Desposito, Anton Fig, Tony Lepore, Cindy Lopez, and Todd Shea. The band has also shared bills with acts like Ted Poley, The Scofflaws, and The Ice Cold Killers.

About the Band

The Nat Funk All Stars are an original power-packed hard rock funk outfit originating from New York. Blending heavy guitar riffs, roaring organ grooves, and tight rhythmic arrangements, the group balances raw energy with dynamic live contrast—switching from full-throttle rock assault to stripped-back, quiet breakdowns on a dime.

Driven by heavy organ lines, twin-guitar textures, and aggressive grooves, the band delivers a distinct sound built on solid musicianship and unpredictable live performance energy.

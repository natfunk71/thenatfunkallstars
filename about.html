The Nat Funk All Stars

Original Hard Rock & Heavy Funk from New York

Band Overview

The Nat Funk All Stars are a high-decibel, original hard rock and heavy funk outfit rooted in New York. Built on a foundation of aggressive rhythm sections, soaring electric guitar work, and grinding Hammond B3 organ lines, the group delivers a distinct sonic experience that bridges late-60s heavy psychedelic funk with 70s hard rock grit.

Origins & History

Formed as a vehicle for unfiltered original songwriting, The Nat Funk All Stars evolved out of the dense New York music scene, bringing together seasoned players from the realm of rock, blues, and live production. Built to operate as a hard-hitting showband, the project was conceived around dynamic arrangements—moving seamlessly from wall-of-sound rock riffs into tight, syncopated funk grooves without dropping the intensity.

Musical Style & Sound

The core sound of the band is defined by its hybrid instrumentation and heavy stage presence:

  • Heavy Keys & Organ: Overdriven Hammond organ, vintage Rhodes, and keyboards driving the harmonic foundation with raw, percussive energy.
  • Hard Rock Lead & Rhythm Guitars: Tube-driven guitar work utilizing fingerpicking dynamics, tube screamer grit, wah pedals, and dual-guitar textures.
  • Driving Bass & Drum Pocket: A relentless low-end engine anchored by heavy bass grooves and steady, pocket-focused drum track arrangements.
  • Dynamic Range: A signature ability to shift from full-bore hard rock volume to sudden, whisper-quiet rhythmic breakdowns on a dime.

Live Performance & Stage Craft

Known for an uncompromising approach to live sound, the band brings Front-of-House production standards directly to the stage. Utilizing clean direct-injection routing, dedicated tube power, and classic dynamic microphone setups, the live show delivers pristine clarity at heavy volume. Whether playing outdoor festivals, regional venues, or intimate club dates, the focus remains on crowd engagement, musical precision, and raw performance power.

Discography & Notable Tracks

The band's expanding catalog features a mix of high-energy instrumental showcases and vocal-driven rock tracks:

  • The Electric Fire Escape (EP) – The flagship studio release featuring full-band arrangements and heavy organ leads.
  • The Coffee Truck Is Here – A fan-favorite groove driven by syncopated bass and rhythm guitar interplay.
  • Nat Funk on the Organ – A featured showcase highlighting overdriven organ improvisation and dynamic breakdown sections.
  • Blue Shoes Have No Place in Outer Space – A psychedelic hard-rock journey combining space-age synthesizer elements with heavy guitar riffs.
  • Nat Funk's Lament – A soulful, slow-burning blues-rock track highlighting deep dynamic shifts.
  • Mud Foot – A low-end heavy funk track built around a thick, distorted bassline and driving drum pocket.
  • Suck The Poison Out – A fast-paced, hard rock assault featuring aggressive vocal hooks and twin guitar leads.
  • Sneaky Pete – A mid-tempo funk rock track packed with subtle guitar fills and organ comping.

In the Studio

In addition to live performances, the band utilizes modern production tools, hybrid direct-analog tracking, and custom audio engineering to continuously test arrangements and produce new material. With an extensive vault of unreleased riffs and demo sessions, new releases are regularly prepared for streaming and physical distribution.
